Card Lookup

Beta. This is an early public version of a tool built for my own team. Not all of the wording may be right, including the notes Claude wrote into it. Grades and projections are from cwhit's public boards (dated on each card); real lines are pooled over every team in the tournament runs I have loaded. Card art is the game's.

Type a card. Grade is how he ranks among cards of his tier at his job on cwhit's boards. Below that: his ratings, his standing on each tournament's board, his real lines in tournament play, and the story of the real player.

How grades workThe blend of his percentiles across cwhit's fresh boards at his own level (Cap boards never grade; his best board is named beside it), then his standing on each tournament's board. The older Quick board stands in where no event board holds him, and the ratings curve where cwhit has no row (marked provisional). Scale: S top 1%, A+ 3%, A 6%, A- 10%, B+ 15%, B 22%, B- 30%, C+ 40%, C 55%, C- 70%, D 85%, F the rest.
